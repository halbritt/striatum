# Branch 5 Actuary Ideas
author: diverger-reviewer-1-002

Frame: Build the loss distribution: probability band x impact multiplier per failure; price the <0.1%, >10x tail.

- **Reserve tickets:** Every accepted arc carries a reserve line for expected rework, verifier churn, stale-context replacement, and slice-discovery overflow; the reserve stays open until proof closes it.
- **Catastrophe exclusions:** Red doctor, authority mismatch, unanchored artifacts, wrong-branch integration, and irreversible source actions are excluded losses; the supervisor stops instead of trying to price them.
- **Experience-rated autonomy:** Future meta-agent authority tightens or loosens by RFC family based on prior claim history: deferral reversals, recovery incidents, stale success disputes, and handoff misses.
- **Tail-event reinsurance lanes:** A campaign keeps scarce independent lanes in reserve for rare >10x failures: provenance corruption, hidden blocked RFCs, silent deferral acceptance, and runner wedge recovery.
- **Premium shock dashboard:** The portfolio view shows the marginal risk premium of adding one more active arc, making the turn from cheap parallelism to concentrated tail exposure visible.
- **Deductible stop conditions:** Human checkpoints act as deductibles: routine coordination losses are absorbed by the supervisor, while predeclared high-impact classes force human payment before the arc continues.
