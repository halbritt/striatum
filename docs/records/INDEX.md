# Records index (striatum)

`docs/records/` holds frozen provenance that is not part of the current
operator surface or the curated reference set. It is not the home for audit
reports anymore; browse whole-repo audits in [`../audits/`](../audits/).

| Kind | Path | Files | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frozen | [`_frozen/`](_frozen/) | Historical per-issue / per-review / design archive (was `docs/_archive/`), frozen research notes, and retained incubation provenance. |

## Not folded here (intentional)

`docs/operator/` and `docs/campaigns/` are **not** in `docs/records/`. They are
runtime/workflow surfaces with path contracts. `docs/audits/` is also not here:
audits are browseable reports with their own top-level README.
